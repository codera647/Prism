Prism 0.5.0 — Windows desktop

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Open Prism.exe. Keep its _internal folder beside it.
3. Create a local account. Start a campaign brief or upload your own ad images.
4. Campaign studio has three views: Campaign brief, References, Creative concepts.
   The green/gray dashboard lists campaigns; Agent Reach searches public sources.
5. Optional Gemini requires your own key, entered privately in AI connection.
   Concept cards can draft two editable product-design prompts.
   Hosted Google accounts and permanent keys require separate OAuth/D1 setup.
   Hosted image generation needs a Muapi key and provider credits.

Requires Windows 10/11 x64 and Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.
Python and a paid AI API account are not required.
The verified CLIP model is included for offline inference.

Your desktop boards and accounts are stored in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Prism\data
Closing the window shuts down its private local server.

Desktop and hosted accounts/boards are separate. No automatic sync is provided.
Reports suggest experiments; they do not predict advertising performance.
See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for bundled components.
